Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Does Elevated Excavating work with general contractors and home builders?

Yes — we specialize in large-scale site work for general contractors, developers, and home builders: subdivision grading, road building, and utility infrastructure for projects too big for a one-truck outfit.

What areas does Elevated Excavating serve?

We serve Spokane, WA and North Idaho, including Coeur d’Alene, Post Falls, and Hayden, along with the surrounding Inland Northwest region.

What kind of excavation work do you handle?

Mass grading and site balancing, land clearing and grubbing, road building and subgrade prep, underground water/sewer/storm utilities, stormwater management, and building pad prep — full-phase subdivision development from raw ground to finished lots.

How do I get a bid for my project?

Call 208.292.8037 or send project details to info@elevatedexcavatingid.com and we’ll schedule a site walk.
