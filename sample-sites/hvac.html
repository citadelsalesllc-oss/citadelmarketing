Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you repair both furnaces and air conditioners?

Yes — we service and repair furnaces, central AC, heat pumps, and ductless mini-split systems, plus install new equipment when a repair no longer makes sense.

What areas does Alpine Comfort Heating & Air serve?

We serve homeowners in Post Falls, Coeur d’Alene, Hayden, Rathdrum, and the surrounding North Idaho and Spokane, WA area.

Is emergency heating or cooling service available?

Yes — a no-heat call in January or a dead AC in August doesn’t wait for a Monday appointment. Call our main line any time for emergency dispatch.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes — a twice-yearly tune-up plan (spring AC check, fall furnace check) catches small problems before they become a no-heat call in the middle of winter.
