Frequently Asked Questions.

Still have a question? Call or text 208.555.0163.

Do you work on homeowner-scale projects, or only big commercial jobs?

Homeowner-scale is our focus — septic systems, driveways, land clearing, and drainage on residential lots. We’re set up for the single-lot job, not just subdivision-sized work.

What areas does Timberline Excavation & Site Work serve?

We serve homeowners and small builders in Hayden, Coeur d’Alene, Post Falls, Rathdrum, Athol, and the surrounding North Idaho and Spokane, WA area.

Are you licensed for septic system installation?

Yes — we’re licensed and bonded for septic system installation and repair in Idaho, and coordinate directly with the county health district on permits and inspections.

How do I get an estimate for my property?

Call 208.555.0163 or send a few details and photos to office@timberlineexcavationid.com and we’ll schedule a free on-site walk.

Will your equipment damage my yard getting to the work site?

We match equipment size to the job and the access we have — that’s the whole point of a right-sized fleet. We’ll walk the access route with you before we start.

Do you haul away debris and spoils?

Yes — hauling and disposal is included in most scopes. We’ll confirm what’s included when we quote the job.

What’s your payment schedule on a project?

Typically a deposit to schedule the work and the balance due on completion. Larger multi-day jobs may have a simple milestone schedule, laid out before we start.
