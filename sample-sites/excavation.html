Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you work on homeowner-scale projects, or only big commercial jobs?

Homeowner-scale is our focus — septic systems, driveways, land clearing, and drainage on residential lots. We’re set up for the single-lot job, not just subdivision-sized work.

What areas does Timberline Excavation & Site Work serve?

We serve homeowners and small builders in Hayden, Coeur d’Alene, Post Falls, Rathdrum, and the surrounding North Idaho and Spokane, WA area.

Are you licensed for septic system installation?

Yes — we’re licensed and bonded for septic system installation and repair in Idaho, and coordinate directly with the county health district on permits and inspections.
