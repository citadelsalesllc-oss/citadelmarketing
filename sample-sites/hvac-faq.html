Frequently Asked Questions.

Still have a question? Call or text 208.555.0187 — a real person answers.

Do you repair both furnaces and air conditioners?

Yes — we service and repair furnaces, central AC, heat pumps, and ductless mini-split systems, plus install new equipment when a repair no longer makes sense.

What areas does Alpine Comfort Heating & Air serve?

We serve homeowners in Post Falls, Coeur d’Alene, Hayden, Rathdrum, Dalton Gardens, and the surrounding North Idaho and Spokane, WA area.

Is emergency heating or cooling service available?

Yes — a no-heat call in January or a dead AC in August doesn’t wait for a Monday appointment. Call our main line any time for emergency dispatch.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes — a twice-yearly tune-up plan (spring AC check, fall furnace check) catches small problems before they become a no-heat call in the middle of winter.

Do you offer financing on new systems?

Yes — $0 down financing is available on qualifying furnace and heat pump installs, plus rebates up to $1,200. Ask when you get your estimate.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, check, and all major credit cards. Payment is due when the work is finished, not before.

Do you handle permits and inspections on new installs?

Yes — we pull the required permits and coordinate inspections on any full system install, so you don’t have to.
